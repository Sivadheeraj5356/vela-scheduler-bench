Scheduler Bench

A small, verifiable eval environment for scheduling agents. Six scenarios over the week of Mon Nov 2, 2026 (US DST has ended): two-party timezone pairs, a three-zone panel, a reschedule, a hard no-Friday rule, and a soft morning preference. An agent proposes a UTC slot; the grader checks it against every participant's real calendar and returns a staged 0-1 reward: 0.2 well-formed and correct duration, 0.6 every required participant free, 0.2 soft preferences. Hard constraints (the scheduling week, banned weekdays) are a gate: a violation scores 0. Timezone conversion was applied when this data was generated, so grading here is pure interval math and matches the Python runner case for case.
